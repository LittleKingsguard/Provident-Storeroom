# Part — `constraint-identity` (`U-STOREROOM-HELD-LISTENER-IDENTITY`, prefix `D-GP-SHLI`): a constraint's identity as a store-side fact, the one act that deletes it, and the pair's set-valued key

- **Status:** **CARRIER PART of this change's contract directory `docs/specs/storeroom-held-listener-identity/`** — authored at the gate-2 spec gate **in the same pass** as the contract document's index region and part map, whose `constraint-identity` row declares it **KIND `carrier`** at the path `docs/specs/storeroom-held-listener-identity/constraint-identity.md`. **It declares nothing the contract does not already carry; it EXPANDS `contract.md §2.6` (`HL-6`).**
- **What this part is.** **Where a CONSTRAINT's identity is declared: its carrier's move from a JS cache into the store, the observable that move preserves, the one act that deletes it, the pair's shape over N addresses, and the set-valued key that shape forces.**

- **The layer.** **Every observable in this part is `ENVELOPE/PURE`. NO `APP`-GREEN IS CLAIMED.** `[CONTRACT]`

## §1. The identity's carrier moves, and what that retires

1. **THE RULING, VERBATIM: *"Move the identity into the store."*** (`P: docs/pending.md · (AA-2)`, verbatim). **`PAR-JS-25`'S IDENTITY BECOMES A STORE-SIDE FACT, RETURNED WITH THE PAIR, AND NO JS CACHE IS LOAD-BEARING FOR IT** (`P: docs/pending.md · (AA-2)`, verbatim). `[CONTRACT]`
2. **THE THREE THINGS THE MOVE RETIRES, EACH NAMED AT ITS OWN BYTES.**
   **(i) `(Y-2)`'s CARRIER READING.** **The cache keyed by `JSON.stringify(singleAddress)` (`bindings/js/index.mjs:2944`, `:2950`) is no longer the identity's home, so the SET-ADDRESS KEY PROBLEM `(Z-2)` raised DISSOLVES with the carrier** (`P: docs/pending.md · (AA-2)`, item (i), verbatim).
   **(ii) THE SINGLE-SILENT-FAILURE FINDING.** **A cache miss is no longer indistinguishable from a removal, because the identity's presence IS a store row's presence** (`P: docs/pending.md · (AA-2)`, item (ii), verbatim).
   **(iii) `(M-3)`'s PROPOSAL THAT `kernel.pairs` KEY ON `id` ALONE.** **`id` is now the identity's own key, since the store holds one row per pair** (`P: docs/pending.md · (AA-2)`, item (iii), verbatim). `[CONTRACT]`
3. **AND THE ONE THING THE MOVE DOES NOT RETIRE, PRINTED BECAUSE IT IS THE PART OF `X-1` THAT SURVIVED.** **`X-1` PUT THE CRUX AS A CHOICE BETWEEN READING (a) *"the identity survives the purge"* and READING (b) *"the identity is re-minted"*, and it recorded that `PAR-JS-25`'s `alt` cell PRICED (b) AND REFUSED IT.** **`(AA-2)` ANSWERS IT WITH READING (a) — the identity is a store row and no purge of a handle's listener entries reaches it — so `PAR-JS-25`'s observable stands and the `alt`'s refusal is not undone** (`P: docs/pending.md · (X-1)`, verbatim; `P: docs/pending.md · (AA-2)`, its own reading). **THE THREE TESTS `X-1` NAMED AS GOING RED — `constraints-publication.test.mjs:546`'s `second === first`, `:1028`'s same assertion, and `constraint-kernel.test.mjs:77`'s store-handle assertion — ARE THEREBY NOT RED, and their authors re-derive them against the moved carrier rather than against a new answer** (`P: docs/pending.md · (X-1)`, verbatim). `[CONTRACT]`

## §2. The store's pair record, and `addConstraint`'s answer at a held pair

1. **THE STORE'S PAIR RECORD CARRIES THE IDENTITY.** **`addConstraint` at a held pair answers THE STORE'S IDENTITY for that pair, preserving `PAR-JS-25`'s observable** (`P: docs/pending.md · (AA-2)`, its own obligation, verbatim: *"the store's pair record carries the identity; `addConstraint` at a held pair answers THE STORE'S IDENTITY for that pair (preserving `PAR-JS-25`'s observable)"*). `[CONTRACT]`
2. **THE OBSERVABLE THE MOVE PRESERVES, IN `PAR-JS-25`'S OWN WORDS.** **The identity is *"equal to itself on a second `addConstraint` at the SAME pair"*** (`P: docs/pending.md · (X-1)`'s head clause, quoting `PAR-JS-25`; `docs/specs/storeroom-js/parameter-semantics.md §2.25`). **ITS TWO PROPERTIES, EACH DRIVEN: (a) a second `addConstraint` at the same pair returns a value EQUAL TO the first; (b) two pairs return TWO values that are NOT equal to each other.** **AND ITS THIRD PROPERTY, UNMOVED: the value carries NO OWN ENUMERABLE MEMBER — a drive compares identities and never contents** (`docs/specs/storeroom-js/surface.md §2.12` item 4, verbatim: *"THIS UNIT DECLARES NO SPELLING FOR IT, NO ACCESSOR REACHING A MEMBER OF IT, AND A DRIVE COMPARES IDENTITIES AND NEVER CONTENTS"*). `[CONTRACT]`
3. **AND THE ONE PLACE `constraints()` TOUCHES THIS AND MUST NOT: THE IDENTITY IS NOT ENUMERATED.** **`a.constraints()` returns `Array<{address, id}>` — THE PAIR and NOT the identity — and an enumeration that surfaced an identity would falsify the opacity declaration the moment a consumer read one out of the array** (`docs/specs/storeroom-js/surface.md §2.12` item 4, verbatim; `bindings/js/index.mjs:3105-3128`'s four exclusions). **Under this part the enumeration's `address` member becomes a SET (`§4`), and its exclusion of `identity` does not move.** `[CONTRACT]`

## §3. The one act that deletes an identity

1. **THE RULING, VERBATIM: *"`removeConstraint` removes the row and the identity with it — WHICH IS NOW THE ONE ACT THAT DELETES AN IDENTITY"*** (`P: docs/pending.md · (AA-2)`, closing sentence; `contract.md §2.6` item 3). `[CONTRACT]`
2. **THE TWO OBSERVABLES A DRIVE ASSERTS, EACH IN ITS OWN TERMS.** **(i) AFTER `removeConstraint(pair)`, A FRESH `addConstraint` AT THAT PAIR ANSWERS A NEW IDENTITY.** **(ii) THE IDENTITY THE CALLER HELD BEFORE RESOLVES TO NOTHING** — that is, no store row carries it and no read of any declared member produces it. `[CONTRACT]`
3. **AND THE TWO ACTS THAT DO NOT DELETE AN IDENTITY, NAMED SO THEY ARE NOT MISTAKEN FOR IT.** **`constraintOff` is a TOGGLE and not a removal: it re-registers a parked stub and KEEPS the pair carried, so the identity STANDS** (`bindings/js/index.mjs:3082`/`:3118`; `docs/specs/storeroom-js/surface.md §2.12` item 5, verbatim: *"`constraintOff` IS THE DISABLE HALF OF A TOGGLE, NOT A REMOVAL"*). **A PURGE OF A HANDLE'S LISTENER ENTRIES does not reach the identity either, because the identity is the store's PAIR row and the purge reaches LISTENER entries** (`§1` item 3; `contract.md §2.2` item 4). `[CONTRACT]`

## §4. The pair over N addresses: one identity, N rows, and the set-valued key

1. **THE RULINGS, VERBATIM: *"One pair, N registry rows, removal clears all N"*** and ***"1. (one id, N addresses)"*** (`P: docs/pending.md · P-7`'s keying ruling and its answer clause). **SO ONE PAIR ELEMENT REGISTERED OVER N ADDRESSES IS ONE PAIR: ONE ID, N ADDRESSES, AND `constraints()` RETURNS ONE ROW FOR IT** (`P: docs/pending.md · P-7`'s answer clause, verbatim). `[CONTRACT]`
2. **THE FIVE SITES THE RULING NAMES, EACH ITS OWN OBLIGATION.** **(i) `withdrawRegistration`'s body changes from ONE `byAddress` delete to N (`bindings/js/src/constraint-kernel.mjs:460-471`; the `kernel.pairs.delete(key)` at `:463` stays one) — and under `HL-8`'s deletion of `byAddress` the N rows it clears are the N `kernel.pairs` rows the pair owns; (ii) `evaluate`'s `pair === undefined` skip at `:1166-1167` becomes UNREACHABLE on the ordinary path, and the disposition — deleted or retained as a defensive arm — is `OWED`; (iii) the pair's N rows are N `pairKey`s sharing ONE id — `pairKey` is `addressToken(address) + '#' + id` (`:220-222`), so N addresses yield N distinct keys naming one element, and the identity is the `id`, never the key — and under `HL-8`'s deletion of `addressToken` the key's address half is `id`-alone, an obligation this part restates; (iv) `carriesConstraintAt` (`bindings/js/index.mjs:3143-3155`)'s `JSON.stringify(address)` key and `entry.addressKey` (`:2944`) must answer TRUE where ANY of the pair's N addresses matches — the per-write gate is a SET-MEMBERSHIP test, not an equality test; (v) the kernel's pair registry keeps its `(address, id)` key shape, because one pair over N addresses IS N `(address, id)` rows sharing an id** (`P: docs/pending.md · P-7`'s keying ruling, items (i)–(v), verbatim). `[CONTRACT]`
3. **`ConstraintPair`'S DECLARED SHAPE MOVES.** **`bindings/js/index.d.ts:630-633` declares `readonly address: Address` with `id: string`; the set-valued form makes `address` an `Address[]`, and `enumerateConstraints` (`bindings/js/index.mjs:3129-3139`) pushes `{address: entry.address.slice(), id: entry.id}` — one row per `state.constraints` VALUE, unchanged in count, with its `address` member's type moved** (`P: docs/pending.md · P-7`'s answer clause, item (1), verbatim). **AND THE ENUMERATION'S OWN UNDECLARED ORDER DOES NOT MOVE: the array is `Map` iteration order and nothing else, and a drive compares the pairs as a SET and never as a sequence** (`docs/specs/storeroom-js/surface.md §2.12` item 6; `DD-22`). `[CONTRACT]`
4. **THE SET-VALUED KEY, DECLARED — because the shape forces it and no ruling supplies it.** **`(Z-2)`'s own words: *"a set key needs order-insensitive equality, which `P-7` itself flags and no ruling supplies"*** (`P: docs/pending.md · (Z-2)`, verbatim). **THIS CONTRACT DECLARES THE RULE: TWO ADDRESS SETS ARE EQUAL EXACTLY WHEN THEY CONTAIN THE SAME ADDRESSES — SET EQUALITY — AND THE CANONICAL KEY IS THE SORTED, INJECTIVELY-SPELLED MEMBER LIST** (`contract.md §2.6` item 6's `HL-DD-3`; `listen-verb.md §2.2` item 5). **The injective spelling is the one the address domain already admits: each segment length-prefixed, with `string` and `number` distinguished** — **the same distinction `addressToken`'s `k:`/`i:` prefixes were carrying, preserved as a spelling rule and not as a map key** (`P: docs/pending.md · (M-3)`, item (iv), verbatim: *"the same distinction `addressToken`'s `k:`/`i:` prefixes were carrying"*). **`alt`, priced: DECLARE A CANONICAL ORDER** — priced: it mints an order guarantee `DD-37`/`DD-22` do not carry and makes a registration's position observable; **available only with `DD-37` reversed by its own instrument.** **Reversal point: a fresh ruling recorded as an ACTIVE `docs/decisions.md` row, followed by a bounded amendment.** `[DECLARED-DEFAULT]`
5. **AND THE ONE DEDUPE THE SET-VALUED PAIR FORCES ON THE PASS.** **A pair at N addresses is reachable by N distinct prefix selections, so the kernel's pass must evaluate the element ONCE per write even where two of its addresses both prefix-match the written address; without it, a two-address pair whose addresses are ancestor and descendant of one write address evaluates twice, breaching `K-3`'s one-pass-per-pair rule** (`P: docs/pending.md · P-7`'s answer clause, item (4), verbatim; `contract.md §2.8` item 1 and `§5.4`'s `S-SEL-DEDUPE`). `[CONTRACT]`
6. **AND THE ONE THING `(AA-2)` LOOSENS, STATED SO IT IS NOT READ AS A WIDENING: `entry.addressKey` MAY STAND AS A CACHE-SIDE SPELLING, BUT IT IS NOT THE IDENTITY'S CARRIER AND NO CLAUSE MAY READ AN IDENTITY OUT OF IT.** **The per-write gate's set-membership test (`§4` item 2 (iv)) is the key's only declared duty** (`P: docs/pending.md · (AA-2)`; `contract.md §2.6` item 1). `[NEGATIVE]`

## §5. The states and the fail states this part declares

| # | The state / fail state | Its condition | Its declared answer | The observable a drive reads |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `IF-1` | a second `addConstraint` at a HELD pair | the pair stands | THE SAME identity, returned | `addConstraint`'s two returns, compared by value |
| `IF-2` | two `addConstraint` calls at two DIFFERENT pairs | two pairs | TWO identities, not equal | the two returns, compared by value |
| `IF-3` | an identity whose contents a consumer reads | any own enumerable member on the identity | none — the identity carries NO own enumerable member | `Object.keys` over the returned identity |
| `IF-4` | a `removeConstraint` then a fresh `addConstraint` at the same pair | a real removal | A DIFFERENT identity | the before and after returns, compared by value |
| `IF-5` | a `constraintOff`/`constraintOn` cycle | a toggle, not a removal | THE SAME identity | the returns before and after the cycle |
| `IF-6` | a purge of a handle's listener entries over a held pair | an app `listen` at the pair's address | THE SAME identity — the purge reaches listener entries and not the pair row | the return after the purge, compared with the return before it |
| `IF-7` | one pair over N addresses | N addresses in one creation | ONE pair, ONE identity, N registry rows, ONE row from `constraints()` | the enumeration's element count; the pair's `address` member's length |
| `IF-8` | a pair whose two address sets are equal but spelled in different member orders | a set key computed by sequence | EQUAL — set equality and not sequence equality | the per-write gate's answer at each of the pair's addresses |
| `IF-9` | a two-address pair, one address an ancestor of the write's and one the write's own | one write | ONE evaluation per pass | the pass's `constraint-check` entry count for that pair (`TF-5`'s sibling in `trigger-and-pair.md §8`) |

**AND THE ONE ROW THAT IS A FAILURE AND NOT A STATE: `IF-3`.** **An identity with a readable member is a falsification of `PAR-JS-25`'s opacity and not a variation, and the drive that finds one has found the defect** (`docs/specs/storeroom-js/surface.md §2.12` item 4). `[CONTRACT]`
