## §5. The conflict test: its seat, its identity keying, and its form

1. **THE CONFLICT IS ONE DIRECTION AND ITS SEAT IS `register`.** **A constraint/listener conflict arises AT `addConstraint` AT AN ADDRESS A LISTENER IS ALREADY REGISTERED AT** (`P: docs/pending.md · (C-1)`, verbatim; `contract.md §2.3` item 8). **THE TEST'S SEAT IS `registerOn` (`bindings/js/index.mjs:2596-2623`), BEFORE `state.registry.set` (`:2621`), so a refusal leaves the registry untouched** (`P: docs/pending.md · (C-2)`, item (i), verbatim). `[CONTRACT]`
