# `cell` — the append's frame, the checklist of statements the appended item must carry, and the closed list of forms it may not take (`carrier`)

- **Status:** **SPEC — a `carrier` part of `docs/specs/storeroom-constraint-outcome/contract.md`, authored in the SAME PASS as that index region. Dated 2026-10-30.** **It drafts no cell text** — the item's sentences are the IMPLEMENTER's, at gate 3 — **it reprints no clause of the target document, it asserts no behaviour, and it raises no `OWED` of its own.** Its every row resolves through exactly ONE target, printed in the row's own citation cell.

## §1 · THE APPEND'S FRAME — five items, each a REQUIREMENT on the appended item's form

| # | The frame item | The ONE citation that resolves it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1** | **INSERTION POINT.** The item's **first byte follows the last byte of the last sub-item printed in `§0.1` item 5**, and its **last byte precedes that section's close**, so the item is the final sub-item of the `OWED` enumeration and of no other enumeration. | `docs/specs/storeroom-constraints/contract.md:360-362` |
| **2** | **ORDINAL.** The item's identifier is the enumeration's **next numeral in its own sequence**, taken exactly as the two sub-items added after that enumeration's first printing took theirs: **no new id, no new prefix and no romanisation of its own.** | `docs/specs/storeroom-constraints/contract.md:347` |
| **3** | **READ-THROUGH MARK, AND NO REPRINT.** The item **names the sub-items it stands beside by ordinal only** and reprints **no run of their bytes**; the reconciliation with the enumeration sentence already standing there lives in the appended item, because that sentence's own bytes may not be touched (`contract.md §2` requirement `2`). | `docs/specs/storeroom-constraints/contract.md:338-346` |
| **4** | **HEAD, BLOCK AND CLOSING SHAPE.** A **dated** head naming the pass and the grant it acts on; **one contiguous block** with no sub-table, no register row and no strategy id; and a close carrying, in order, its **OWNER**, its **positive revisit condition**, **what it does not block**, and the mark that **no clause may be read as settling it** — the shape the enumeration's last added sub-item already takes. | `docs/specs/storeroom-constraints/contract.md:347-360` |
| **5** | **IDENTIFIER, TERMINATED.** The item's identifier is the ordinal of item `2` and **nothing else**: it enters **no row of the enumeration's `RC-` table**, mints no token, and carries no prefix. | `docs/specs/storeroom-constraints/contract.md:330-336` |

## §2 · THE CHECKLIST — eleven slots, in this order. Each slot states ONE thing the item must assert; the fact itself is pinned in `§2` of the contract and is not restated here.

| Slot | What the slot must assert, and nothing more | Discharges |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1** | **the head:** the date, the appending pass, and the grant the item acts on. | `contract.md §2` requirement `1` |
| **2** | **the subject:** one returned shape answers **two different things** at the two instants, named as the two instants and not as two code sites. | `contract.md §2` requirement `3` |
| **3** | **the landing instant's read**, cited at its **live range**. | `contract.md §2` requirement `3` |
| **4** | **the registration instant's read**, cited at its **live range**, **with the comment beside it that asserts the symmetry the code falsifies**. | `contract.md §2` requirement `4` |
| **5** | **every citation the item prints is live** — a resolving path, a resolving row id, or a name the project owns. | `contract.md §2` requirement `5` |
| **6** | **the two rulings the item cites by id**, each of which resolves in the authority log. | `contract.md §2` requirement `6` |
| **7** | **the classification:** the site is not an unruled gap but a **contradiction of a closure** already declared closed as implemented. | `contract.md §2` requirement `7` |
| **8** | **the provenance** that places the read inside the class that closure closed, and the word **REGRESSION** for what the site therefore is. | `contract.md §2` requirement `8` |
| **9** | **the OWNER**, as a **class of unit named by its shape** and by no unit number. | `contract.md §2` requirement `10` |
| **10** | **the positive revisit condition**, as **an event that will occur and is checkable**. | `contract.md §2` requirement `11` |
| **11** | **the item's bounds on itself:** the divergence is **RECORDED and NOT CURED**; it declares **no arm** of the closed outcome set; it **repairs no other citation**; it **re-derives no closure predicate**. | `contract.md §2` requirement `18` |

## §3 · THE FORMS THE APPENDED ITEM MAY NOT TAKE — seven items, closed. Each is a prohibition on the item's bytes; the check that detects each is `checks.md §1`.

| # | The forbidden form | Ground |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1** | **Naming a repair.** The token `repair` may appear **only as the outcome's own member name**; no clause of the item may name an act, a change, a patch or a correction that would fix the divergence. | `contract.md §2` requirement `9` |
| **2** | **Asserting the divergence harmless** — or immaterial, benign, acceptable, inconsequential, or of no impact. | `contract.md §2` requirement `9` |
| **3** | **Assigning the instant to another document's clause.** If the neighbouring unit's own clause for the registration instant is mentioned at all, it is mentioned as **NOT** that instant's owner; the item never places the instant there. | `contract.md §2` requirement `9` |
| **4** | **Re-opening or widening `D-35`/`D-36`:** no new class, no second site, no parameter row, no re-reading of either ruling's reach. | `contract.md §2` requirement `9` |
| **5** | **Any statement of the arm set:** the item neither prints the closed outcome's arms, nor counts them, nor restates them as a set — it records the divergence and declares no arm. | `contract.md §2` requirement `13` |
| **6** | **Any archived path and any archived-only row identifier:** no path to an archive or to an absent part, no token of a row whose home is archived, and no bare part filename. | `contract.md §2` requirement `5` |
| **7** | **Any identifier beyond the ordinal:** no `RC-` id, no new id family, no prefix, no new token, and no ruling — this unit files none. | `contract.md §2` requirement `12` |
