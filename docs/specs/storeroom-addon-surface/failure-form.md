## §1. UNCHANGED, AND THAT IS THE CLAUSE

**A refusal crossing the boundary is never an `Error`.** **This unit does not move the form.** The split changes how a verb is reached; **it does not change what a verb answers when it refuses.**

**EVERY ONE OF THE THIRTEEN ENTRIES REFUSES IN THE SAME FORM.** The set widens from five names to thirteen; **the form is one form across all of them.**

**AND THE FORM'S SINGLE HOME IS THE SIBLING UNIT'S PART — `docs/specs/storeroom-addon-adoption/failure-form.md`.** **RESTATING ITS MEMBERS HERE WOULD BE A SECOND HOME FOR THEM, WHICH IS THE DEFECT CLASS THIS REGENERATION EXISTS TO REMOVE.** **This part states only what THIS unit does to the form — which is nothing — and the one member whose value belongs here.** `[CONTRACT]`

## §2. THE ONE MEMBER THIS UNIT OWNS

**`log` IS PART OF THE FORM AND THIS UNIT OWNS NOTHING OF IT.** It is built at the JS boundary and never in Rust.

**THE MEMBER WHOSE VALUE IS `OWED` HERE IS THE REFUSAL ARM'S `status` TOKEN AND ITS `message`.**

| What is decided | What is `OWED` |
| --- | --- |
| the form — three members, no `Error`, the key set | **which member of the store's closed status set the arm answers** |
| the arm — one observable, covering a detached call and a collected store | **the arm's `message` text** |

**BECAUSE THE TOKEN IS `OWED`, THE DRIVES ARE WRITTEN TO WHAT IS DECIDED.** **`P-SAS-4` asserts the arm's SHAPE — the key set, the member count and non-`Error`-ness (**and NOT the absence of a `stack`: `stack` is not part of the form and is not forbidden**) — and asserts NOTHING about which token stands in `status`.** **A drive is authorable today; only the expected value is deferred.**

**AND THE TOKEN'S SET IS CLOSED AND THE BOUNDARY MINTS NO MEMBER OF IT.** Whoever writes the arm chooses **a member**, not a new code. `[OWED]`

---

## §3. WHAT A DRIVE MUST NOT DO

**A DRIVE MUST NOT ASSERT THE ARM'S `status` TOKEN OR ITS `message` WHILE THEY ARE `OWED`.** **Such a cell would pin a value that does not exist**, and it would fail the moment the value is chosen — **for a reason that is not a defect.**

**This is stated as a prohibition because it is the mistake the deferral invites.** The restated failure list at `P-SAS-4` names the shape, the count, the prototype and the outcome, **and stops there deliberately**. `[CONTRACT]`

## §4. THE ONE REFUSAL THIS UNIT ADDS

**The receiver arm — *"the receiver names no live store"* — refuses in this form and in no other.**

**IT ADDS ONE OBSERVABLE AND NO NEW CODE.** The arm's `status` is drawn from the store's **closed set**; **the boundary does not mint a member for it.** `R: the TORN DOWN row`, part (6). `[CONTRACT]`
